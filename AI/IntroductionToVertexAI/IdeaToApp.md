<h3>Everything starts with a prompt.</h3>
<p>When you first use Vertex AI Studio, you tell the AI what you want to do. You can ask a question or give an instruction using natural language.
This is called a <b>prompt</b>.</p>
<h5>Prompt is a natural language request to an AI model.</h5><hr/>
<p>Once the model receives the prompt, it generates text, code, images, videos, music, and more.</p>
<h5>This process of creating prompts to get the response you want is called prompt design.</h5><hr/>

![img_4.png](img_4.png)
<h5>And the iterative process of repeatedly drafting and refining prompts and assessing the model's responses is called prompt engineering.</h5><hr/>

![img_5.png](img_5.png)
<p>Generally, a prompt includes one or more of the following key components-- task, context, and examples.</p>

- <p><b>Task </b> It's what you want the model to do, core of your prompt.Depending on the complexity of the task, you may only need to provide the task itself.<b>This is called zero-shot prompting.</b></p>
- <p><b>Context, often called system instructions: </b>It provides background information, helping the AI model understand the request and generate a more relevant and accurate response.</p>
- <p><b>Examples: </b>You may need to show the AI how to respond. <b>This is known as few-shot prompting.</b></p>
